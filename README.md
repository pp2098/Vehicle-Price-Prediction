# Vehicle-Price-Prediction
Price prediction is a very popular application of machine learning and regression models are typically quite popular in price prediction tasks. The objective of this project is to develop a robust regression model to try and predict used car prices for a reseller based on various listed features and specifications. One is price prediction. So, we can model the car prices based on features such as mileage, fuel type, performance, and so on. Market analysis by exploring trends and preferences in the used car market by type, region, or other metrics and importance of features. 
So what kind of factors most influence car prices? So maybe fuel type or mileage and so on.
Now, doing such an analysis will lead to better understanding and better knowledge of why certain prices work and why certain prices may not work. That is the overall goal. So pricing that is the decision of what the price should be on products is a task in itself. A bad pricing could lead to stagnant deals.A bad pricing could lead to negative effects on sales.So it is important to understand what the right pricing of a product could be.Now, whereas the market itself has forces by which prices could be regulated, such an ML model, if we are able to build an ML model that predicts used car prices accurately, this ML model could then be used to speed up the process of deciding the prices on on various used cars and this is a big help to resellers and dealers and so on.
